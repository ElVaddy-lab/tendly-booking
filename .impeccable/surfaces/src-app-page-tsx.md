# Landing page — `/`

Scope: Tendly marketing landing (Persuade). Audience: Upwork/Freelancehunt evaluators and small barbershop/salon owners. Action: book a real slot at the demo barbershop from the hero; secondary: explore the dashboard as Owner, Manager or Staff. Constraints: demo business labelled fictional; no invented customers, metrics or testimonials; WCAG 2.2 AA.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-1-centered-card.png` (approved 2026-11-15).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A booking page should feel like a calm, full day at the shop; the hero proves it by letting you book a real slot. Refuses the category default of a headline over a static product screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Daylit pale-oak photograph as the page ground; warm off-white panels resting on it with soft long shadows; near-black text; one near-black pill as the only strong accent; light humanist sans display, small neutral sans UI.

STORY: Visitor reads "Keep every chair full and every day calm", sees a barber and a clock living inside the sentence, books 10:30 at Northside Barbers (fictional), then explores the dashboard by role.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating off-white nav pill at top. Centered two-line light headline with an inline barber-photo pill and a clock-10:30 pill. Two-line subline. Below, a wide four-column booking card (service, barber, date, time + Book button). Quiet role line under it; next section's dark edge at the bottom.

FORM: pinned by the user — seed: none (pinned). "Мені подобається 1 і 2. В першому мені подобається як виглядає Hero секція і головний текст на ній з цими об'єктами. А на другому спокійний вигляд дерева і стиль" (references: Cal.com hero, Acuity Scheduling wood).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
